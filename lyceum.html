About Us

A school community built on character and achievement.

We believe every learner deserves a place to thrive, discover their strengths, and grow with confidence.

Lyceum of San Pedro has become a trusted education partner for families who want more than classroom instruction. We focus on developing critical thinking, creativity, leadership, and compassion.

Through engaging lessons, mentorship, and meaningful activities, students are encouraged to become responsible citizens and future-ready professionals.
